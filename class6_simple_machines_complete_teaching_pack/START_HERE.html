Suggested classroom flow

  1. Open the PowerPoint and introduce the concept.
  2. Teach the three lever classes before opening the lever experiment.
  3. Ask students to predict what will happen before changing a control.
  4. Change one setting at a time; observe and explain the result.
  5. Use the quick-check slides and exit ticket to assess understanding.
