TECHNICAL NOTES:

- Lab results are idealized estimates and ignore friction. The lever diagrams show the three part orders; for real experiments, use actual lever arm lengths and physical measurements.
- Textbook editions vary, so verify whether mechanical advantage formulas are part of the required Grade 6 curriculum before assessing them.
