Designed for learning

Let students discover the pattern.

The goal isn't to add screens to every lesson. It's to give students a chance to predict, investigate what changes and explain what they observed.

Classroom note: Start with a physical demonstration when it's safe and practical. Use the simulation to extend observation, not to replace hands-on experience.
01
Demonstrate

Begin with a real object, event or question students can relate to.

02
Ask for a prediction

Invite students to explain what they think will happen before showing the result.

03
Explore and compare

Change one variable in the simulation and notice which outcomes change.

04
Explain and assess

Let students describe the pattern, then connect it to the formal concept and a short assessment.

Work in progress: Some Class 8 instrument visuals are illustrative rather than functional measurement tools, and the geographical-versus-magnetic-pole visualisation needs further refinement. Please verify activities before classroom use. Assessment information containing real student names or scores should never be published here.
